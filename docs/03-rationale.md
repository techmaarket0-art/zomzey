# ZOMZEY homepage — design rationale

**Concept: "Post. Pitch. Paid." — The Opportunity Network**

## The idea in one line
ZOMZEY's own promise is *"Pioneers post, members pitch to win."* The homepage turns that
sentence into the layout: the headline is the product loop, the first screen is a live network
of real-looking opportunities, and the page then follows **one** opportunity from brief to payout
before opening back out to the whole marketplace.

## Why the layout is intentionally different
Most marketplaces stack *hero → feature cards → testimonials → CTA*. That structure explains a
product in the abstract. ZOMZEY's value — three sides meeting, with money held safely in
between — is easier to believe when you **watch it happen**. So the page doesn't have a feature
grid or a "Why choose us" block. It has:

1. **The network** (first screen) — opportunities, members and offers connected by live lines.
2. **The deal record** — one author's brief becomes a single receipt that grows as you scroll:
   posted → four offers → agreed in chat → £180 held → released. This replaces "How it works",
   "Features" and "Trust" in one continuous story.
3. **The index** — "That was one book. The network is bigger." Choose any Pioneer type and lines
   draw to the Influencers and Hubs who typically pitch them.
4. **The live board** — real categories, real budgets, real offer counts, filterable.
5. **Two ways in** — a door for promoters and a door for earners, each with a working tool.
6. **The close** — *"Your next opportunity is already out there."*

## How visitors understand the marketplace in seconds
- **The headline is a three-step legend.** *Post* (free for Pioneers) · *Pitch* (Influencers,
  Hubs & agencies) · *Paid* (Protected Payments, powered by Stripe). Hovering a word highlights its
  part of the network.
- **A shape grammar does the sorting before the reading.** Pioneers and opportunities are
  **square**, people are **round**, places (bookshops, venues, cafés, libraries) have **arched
  doorways**, protection is a **seal**. A visitor can tell a bookshop from a TikTok creator from a
  brief at a glance — everywhere on the page, and later across the product.
- **Variety is visible.** Books, music, venues, food & drink, beauty, apps, travel, kids and courses
  all appear with their own small artefact (a book cover, an EP sleeve, a hot-sauce bottle…), so
  ZOMZEY never reads as "just another influencer marketplace". Hubs — ZOMZEY's real
  differentiator — get equal billing.

## How the two intents are handled
- **First screen:** a two-tab switch — *I need promotion* / *I want to earn* — drives a plain-English
  **sentence builder**: *"I'm an author looking for book clubs in the North West"* or *"I'm a
  bookshop and I can offer shelf & window space."* The CTA changes with the intent (*Find people* /
  *Browse opportunities*).
- **The choice follows you.** The same intent state widens the matching door further down, sets the
  mobile dock's main action (*Post free* vs *Join free*), and is remembered on the next visit.
- **Both doors have a real tool:** Pioneers get a brief starter (what, who should pitch, budget);
  earners get a "What's a feed post worth?" slider based on ZOMZEY's published pricing guide.

## How navigation stays clear
- Desktop: **Discover · Opportunities · How it works · Protected Payments · Tools**, then *Log in*
  and one primary *Join free*. Tools is a simple disclosure menu listing ZOMZEY's five free tools,
  which were previously hard to find.
- Every bold section is anchored and labelled; nothing requires discovering a hidden gesture.
- Mobile: a compact header (logo, *Log in*, menu) and a **thumb-reach dock** that appears once the
  hero's own CTA has scrolled away — *Discover*, *Opportunities* and one contextual action.

## Trust, shown rather than claimed
Every trust cue is placed **where the risk is felt**: "Pay only on a real deal" beside the first
CTA; the £180 *held* seal in the hero; the deal record stamping **Held** then **Released**; verified
seals on members; "Payments processed by Stripe", ad labelling and the ITXL LTD company details in
the footer. All claims are taken from ZOMZEY's own site — no invented member counts, partnerships or
commission rates.

## Why the visual system fits ZOMZEY
- **Warm paper and near-black ink** feel editorial and dependable — closer to a respected publication
  than a startup template, which suits a platform asking people to trust it with money.
- **One electric violet** for actions and connections; **one green** used *only* for protected or
  released money, so the colour itself becomes a trust signal.
- **Bricolage Grotesque** gives the brand a confident, slightly idiosyncratic voice at poster sizes;
  **Geist** keeps the interface calm; **Geist Mono** makes every listing read like a precise ticket.
- **Perforated "ticket" edges** on opportunities and buttons echo the noticeboard tear-off flyer —
  the physical ancestor of "post an opportunity" found in every bookshop, café and venue.

## How mobile changes (not just stacks)
- The headline keeps its three-word legend; the intent switch becomes a full-width segmented control
  under the thumb.
- The network becomes a **swipeable "Live on ZOMZEY" rail** that peeks into the first screen.
- The deal record gains **sticky stage chips** (01 Posted → 05 Released) and offers become a
  swipeable row inside the receipt.
- The index becomes **Pioneer chips** that light up round (person) and arched (place) member chips.
- The board becomes a **feed with a sticky filter rail**; briefs open in a **bottom sheet** with a
  pinned "Pitch for this" action.
- One door at a time, switched at the top; footer links collapse into accordions.

## Key interaction cues
- Offers travel as dots along the hero's connection lines; the receiving slip's avatar pulses.
- Headline words highlight their zone of the network on hover.
- Seals **stamp** onto the receipt as each stage scrolls into view.
- Index lines **draw** from the chosen Pioneer to who pitches.
- Cards lift with an ink border; ticket-stub arrows nudge; focus is always a visible violet ring.
- Motion is calm, pausable (*Pause motion* control on the hero) and fully disabled under
  *prefers-reduced-motion* — the page is already complete without it.

## What is real and what is illustrative
- **Real (from zomzey.io):** terminology (Pioneers, Influencers, Hubs, agencies), categories,
  ZAi, ZBeacon, smart filters, Protected Payments powered by Stripe, verified members, disputes
  covered, "free to join — pay only on a real deal", the five free tools, the pricing-guide
  benchmark, affiliate programme, and ITXL LTD company details. "BrightShelf Books" is ZOMZEY's own
  sample Hub.
- **Illustrative:** individual listings, people, prices and offer counts in the UI fragments. They
  are written to match ZOMZEY's categories and published pricing ranges and are labelled as
  illustrative on the page.
