# 02 — Creative direction

## Three directions explored

### A · "The Switchboard"
The whole page is split vertically into two halves — *I need* / *I offer* — and every
section mirrors itself left and right. Hovering a side widens it.

### B · "The Classifieds"
The homepage as a modern broadsheet of opportunities: a masthead, dense columns of
WANTED / OFFERED listings, editorial rules and a "small print" trust column.

### C · "The Opportunity Network — follow the slip"
A structured, living network on the first screen where **real-looking opportunity slips,
member profiles and protection seals connect to each other**. The headline is the legend of
that network (*Post. Pitch. Paid.*). The rest of the page **follows one opportunity** from
posting to payout, then widens back out to the whole network. Borrows B's editorial
typography for discipline and A's intent switch for clarity — without their gimmicks.

### Scoring (1–5)

| Criterion | A · Switchboard | B · Classifieds | C · Network |
|---|---|---|---|
| Originality | 3 | 4 | **5** |
| Brand fit (opportunity-led, 3 sides) | 3 | 3 | **5** |
| Clarity in 3 seconds | **5** | 3 | 4 |
| Trust (shows the deal mechanics) | 2 | 3 | **5** |
| Mobile adaptability | 2 | 4 | **4** |
| Memorability | 3 | 4 | **5** |
| Implementation quality risk (lower = riskier) | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| **Total** | 22 | 25 | **31** |

A is clear but splits the brand in two and is awkward on phones. B is memorable but reads
retro and loses the social, *living* quality of a network. **C wins**; its one risk —
visual noise — is controlled by a strict shape grammar and a single accent colour.

## The chosen concept

**"Post. Pitch. Paid." — The Opportunity Network.**

*Section order as built: Network (hero) → The deal record (follow one opportunity) → The index
(who pitches whom) → Live board → Two ways in → Close.*

1. **The headline is the product loop.** ZOMZEY's own line is *"Pioneers post, members pitch
   to win."* We compress it into three words and footnote each one with the real mechanic
   (free to post · Influencers, Hubs and agencies send offers · Protected Payments powered by
   Stripe). The words connect to their zone in the live network beside them.
2. **The page follows one opportunity.** The slip that is highlighted in the hero becomes the
   sticky card of the next section and changes state as you scroll: *Posted → Offers →
   Agreed → Held → Released*. This one sequence replaces "how it works", "features" and
   "why trust us".
3. **Then it widens.** "That was one book." An interactive index shows who connects with
   whom (authors ↔ book clubs, musicians ↔ venues, food brands ↔ cafés), a live board shows
   variety, and two doors invite each intent.

## Shape grammar — "Pioneers are square. People are round. Places have doors. Protection is a seal."

| Shape | Means | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| ▭ Square / sharp slip with perforation | The posting side: an opportunity, a deal, money | Listings, offers, briefs, receipts, Pioneer avatars, buttons |
| ● Circle | A person | Influencers, creators, musicians, podcasters |
| ⌂ Arch (doorway) | A place | Hubs — bookshops, venues, cafés, libraries, clubs |
| ✺ Seal | Protection | Verified badges, Protected Payments states |
| ●● Overlapping circles | A roster | Talent agencies |

The grammar makes the marketplace legible at a glance — you can tell a bookshop from a
TikTok creator from a brief before reading a word — and gives ZOMZEY a visual identity
that extends beyond the homepage (profile pages, dashboards, emails, social).

## Visual system

- **Canvas:** warm paper `#F3F0E8`, near-black warm ink `#16140F`.
- **One accent:** Signal Violet `#4A2BEA` — actions, connections, *the network*.
- **One signal:** Release Green `#0A7049` — only for money that is protected, released or
  verified safe.
- **Type (v1.1 polish):** three voices, one job each.
  *Bricolage Grotesque* — headlines only, de-condensed (98–100% width) and lighter: the hero at
  580, every section heading at one shared size and weight (550), so the hero is the single peak.
  *Geist* — everything people scan: body, card and listing titles, labels, buttons, and **all
  money** in tabular figures (a plain £ reads as more trustworthy than a display swash).
  *Geist Mono* — only the deal record's document data (IDs, receipt field labels).
- **Motion:** only where it explains — offers travel along connection lines, the sticky deal
  card changes state, index lines draw between matched sides. Everything is static and
  complete under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Headline decision

The suggested line *"Your next opportunity is already out there."* is good but could
belong to a job board or a dating app. *"Post. Pitch. Paid."* is specific to ZOMZEY's
mechanic, works for both intents, and fits the three-sided network. The suggested line is
kept as the closing statement, where it lands as a promise after the mechanics are proven.
